Device logs

Collect logs from a connected iPhone or iPad to reproduce and diagnose issues.

Find the device

Connect the device, trust the computer, then confirm it is visible.

xcrun devicectl list devices

Stream the live log

Open Console on your Mac and select the device in the sidebar, or stream from the terminal with libimobiledevice. 

brew install libimobiledevice
idevicesyslog | grep "YourApp"

Export crash logs

On the device, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data and share the crash file with the bug report.
