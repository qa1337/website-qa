Simulators

Use simulators to cover the device matrix quickly, then confirm the important flows on real hardware.

Boot and launch

xcrun simctl list devices available
xcrun simctl boot "iPhone 16"
xcrun simctl install booted MyApp.app
xcrun simctl launch booted com.example.app

When to use a real device

  • Camera and sensors.
  • Push notifications on real networks.
  • Performance and battery.
  • Bluetooth and NFC.
