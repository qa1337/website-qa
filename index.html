Principles

Good QA is repeatable. Every guide here follows the same shape: what you need, how to do it, and how to confirm it worked.

Reproduce first. Steps, device, OS and build. If you can't repro it, you can't verify the fix.

Capture evidence. Logs and screenshots beat descriptions.

Confirm the fix. Re-test on the same setup before closing.
